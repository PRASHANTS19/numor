# CA Connect: Chartered Accountant Bookings

## What is CA Connect?
CA Connect is an integrated scheduling and consulting marketplace in Numor. It allows SME users to find, review, and book professional consultation slots with verified Chartered Accountants (CAs).

## How does a CA configure their Profile?
A CA has a specialized profile (`CAProfile`) containing:
-  **Credentials:** Registration number (monitored for regulatory compliance) and years of professional experience.
-  **Billing:** Hourly fee and applicable tax percentages.
-  **Biographical details:** Written bio, languages spoken, and professional specializations (e.g. corporate tax, audit, GST advice).
-  **Integrations:** Social numbers (WhatsApp), calendar setup (Cal.com, Calendly, or Google Calendar sync details).

## How do CAs manage availability slots?
CAs define repeating weekly availability slots via the `CASlot` model:
-  **Scheduling details:** Specific day of the week (Monday through Sunday) and start/end time.
-  **Type of call:** Phone, Video (Zoom/Google Meet), or Both.
-  **Slot Status:** Indicates availability, such as `AVAILABLE`, `HOLD` (temporarily blocked during checkout), or `BOOKED`.

## How does booking a consultation work?
SME Users browse the directory of available CAs, view ratings/reviews, select a slot, and initiate a booking:
1.  **Booking Code:** A unique `CABooking` code is generated, and the slot status transitions to a temporary hold.
2.  **Payment Gateway:** Payment details are handled by `CAPayment`. The booking status stays as `PAYMENT_PENDING` until confirmation is received from the payment gateway.
3.  **Booking Confirmed:** Once paid, the booking transitions to `CONFIRMED` status, and an automated meeting link is generated (incorporating Zoom, Cal.com, or Google Meet integration).
4.  **Google Calendar Sync:** If the CA has connected their Google Calendar account (`googleCalendarConnected` is true), Numor uses the Google Calendar API to automatically schedule the event, create a conference link, and notify both parties.

## What is the Admin approval workflow for CAs?
To guarantee regulatory standards and prevent unverified accountants from consulting:
-  **Pending profile:** When a CA creates or updates their registration details, experience, registration number, or hourly fees, the updates are saved into a pending model (`CAProfilePending` and `CADocumentPending`).
-  **Document Uploads:** CAs upload certification documents or identification proofs (`CADocumentType` including `CERTIFICATION` and `ID_PROOF`).
-  **Admin Review:** A system admin reviews the documents, comments on verification statuses (`caProfile.status` transitions from `PENDING` -> `UNDER_REVIEW`), and approves (`APPROVED`) or rejects (`REJECTED`) the CA. Only approved profiles go live in the public directory.

## How do reviews and ratings work?
After a consultation booking is marked as `COMPLETED`, the SME user can submit a review (`CAReview`). Reviews require a numeric rating (1-5) and an optional comment. Numor recalculates the CA's average rating (`ratingAvg`) and total review count (`ratingCount`) dynamically, helping other users make informed decisions.
